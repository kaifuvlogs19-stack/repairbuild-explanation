Safety by design

Not every repair belongs in a DIY guide.

A credible repair platform must know when to stop giving instructions and route the user to qualified help.

!

Risk-aware guidance

Electrical, battery, gas, and other hazardous faults should trigger clear stop instructions and professional referral where appropriate.

✓

Honest uncertainty

Possible causes should not be presented as certain diagnoses. Device model, symptoms, and verified information affect confidence.

◎

Verified information

Part compatibility, technician credentials, prices, and availability should be shown only when supported by reliable data.
